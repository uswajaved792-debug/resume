Botox

Botox injections are effective in reducing the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles by temporarily relaxing the underlying muscles. This treatment is popular for its quick results and minimal recovery time.

Why it's done

Botox injections are shots that use a toxin to prevent a muscle from moving for a limited time. These shots are often used to smooth wrinkles on the face. They're also used to treat neck spasms, sweating, overactive bladder, lazy eye and other conditions. Botox shots also may help prevent migraine.

Botox shots block certain chemical signals from nerves that cause muscles to contract. The most common use of these injections is to relax the facial muscles that cause frown lines and other facial wrinkles. Botox injections also are used to ease symptoms of some health conditions.

BEFORE CARE

  • Avoid blood thinners (like aspirin, ibuprofen, or Vitamin E) for at least one week prior to treatment.
  • Do not drink alcohol 24 hours before the procedure.
  • Refrain from using any topical retinoids or glycolic acid products for 48 hours prior.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise or heavy workouts for 24 hours before the treatment.
  • Consult with your practitioner if you are pregnant or nursing.

AFTER CARE

  • Avoid touching or massaging the treated area for at least 24 hours after treatment.
  • Refrain from strenuous exercise or heavy workouts for 24 hours post-treatment.
  • Avoid lying down for 4 hours after the procedure.
  • Do not apply makeup for at least 24 hours.
  • Avoid direct sun exposure or tanning beds for at least a week after treatment.
  • Expect some mild swelling or bruising, which should subside within a few days.
