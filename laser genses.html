LASER GENESIS:

Laser Genesis skin therapy is an innovative, technology advanced way to promote vibrant and healthy looking skin in men and women.

The laser genesis helps to regenerate collagen by heating the dermal (the middle layer of skin) part of the skin. It delivers micro–pulses of light to get rid of wrinkles, scars and pigmentation. The micro–pulse technology doesn’t damage the skin surface because the action is really quick.

How does it work?

The Laser Genesis procedure has been scientifically proven to produce new collagen. You can expect to see subtle yet consistent results after each, without unwanted side effects such as bruising or excessive skin irritation. Treatments can be performed in a relaxed, comfortable manner without the use of topical anaesthetics or gel. Laser genesis patients report excellent results over their entire faces and neck. Additionally, practitioners may suggest this therapy to diminish recent scars on other parts of the body.

This treatment requires a minimum of 3 to 6 sessions upon the consultation and your skin concerns. We advise to do maintenance treatment after 6 months or depending on your skin condition,for a rejuvenating boost of collagen. For best results you can combine Laser Genesis with other procedures like Hydrafacial, microdermabrasion, oxygenic facial, Radiofrequency, Chemical peels and Injectables. We advise also to wear sunscreen to protect your skin.

BEFORE CARE

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for at least 2 weeks before your treatment.
  • Refrain from using retinol, glycolic acid, or any exfoliating products for at least 3-5 days prior.
  • Do not use any hair removal treatments, such as waxing or laser, for 1 week before the procedure.
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications (e.g., aspirin, ibuprofen) for 1 week prior to your treatment.
  • Stay well-hydrated in the days leading up to your appointment.
  • Arrive with clean, makeup-free skin on the day of the treatment.
  • Discuss any allergies, skin conditions, or medications with your provider prior to the treatment.
  • Notify your provider if you have any active skin infections or open wounds.

AFTER CARE

  • Avoid washing your face for at least 4 hours after the treatment.
  • Use a gentle, non-soap cleanser and avoid rubbing the treated area.
  • Apply a soothing, hydrating moisturizer as recommended by your provider.
  • Do not pick or peel at the skin; allow it to heal naturally.
  • Refrain from sun exposure and apply broad-spectrum sunscreen daily (SPF 30 or higher).
  • Avoid hot water, steam rooms, saunas, or intense workouts for at least 1 week.
  • Do not use exfoliating products, retinol, or acid-based products until your skin is fully healed.
  • Limit makeup application for 24-48 hours post-treatment.
  • Continue to stay hydrated and drink plenty of water to support skin recovery.
  • Contact your provider if you experience any signs of irritation, unusual discomfort, or adverse reactions.
