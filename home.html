our services

Microblading

Microblading is a semi-permanent makeup technique that involves manually inserting pigment into the upper layers of the skin to create fuller and naturally shaped eyebrows. The effects can last between 12-24 months, depending on skin type, after which the pigment fades, leaving the skin and natural brows as they were. The results are natural and lifelike, giving the brows a fuller appearance.

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) Therapy

This advanced treatment uses the client's blood plasma, rich in growth factors, to rejuvenate the skin, promote healing, and enhance collagen production. PRP therapy is known for its effectiveness in improving skin texture, reducing fine lines and wrinkles, and promoting hair growth.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers are used to restore lost volume, smooth out wrinkles, and enhance facial contours. These injectable treatments provide immediate results with minimal downtime, helping clients achieve a youthful and refreshed appearance.

Botox

Botox injections are effective in reducing the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles by temporarily relaxing the underlying muscles. This treatment is popular for its quick results and minimal recovery time.

Chemical Peels

Chemical peels involve applying a solution to the skin to exfoliate the outer layers, revealing fresher, smoother, and more youthful skin underneath. They are effective in treating acne scars, pigmentation, and fine lines.

Microneedling

Microneedling is a minimally invasive treatment that involves using fine needles to create tiny punctures in the skin. This stimulates the body's natural healing process, promoting collagen and elastin production for improved skin texture and tone.
