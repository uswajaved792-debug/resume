HydraFacial:

HydraFacial is effective for every skin type and addresses specific skin concerns, such as anti-aging, fine lines and wrinkles, acne and congested and pigmentation. It has no downtime or discomfort and uses patented advanced technology, to deliver botanical ingredients with some of the most desirable skin nutrients, such as hyaluronic acid, horse chestnut seed, red algae extract, copper, zinc and magnesium peptides, deeply into the skin to plump it up and deliver radiance to the skin.

How does HydraFacial work?

HydraFacial uses a Vortex tip to create a gentle suction that removes dead skin cells and extracts impurities from the skin. This removes dirt and debris from the skin to unclog pores and hydrate the skin. The result is an immediate, visible improvement of the skin’s radiance and hydration. You will feel a gentle, rough pressure but should not expect to experience discomfort. HydraFacial can be safely used on acne patients to calm existing breakouts and prevent new breakouts from happening. The treatment typically takes 30-45 minutes.

BEFORE CARE

  • Avoid direct sun exposure and tanning for at least 2 weeks before the treatment.
  • Do not use any exfoliating products or treatments (like retinoids or glycolic acid) for 3 days prior.
  • Discontinue any use of blood thinners (like aspirin or ibuprofen) a few days before.
  • Avoid facials and skin treatments for at least one week before your appointment.
  • Consult with your practitioner if you are pregnant or have any skin conditions.

AFTER CARE

  • Immediate glow and improved overall complexion, leaving skin looking fresh and revitalized.
  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for at least 2 weeks post-treatment.
  • Do not use any exfoliating products for at least 3-5 days after the treatment.
  • Hydrate your skin by drinking plenty of water and using a gentle moisturizer.
  • Avoid hot showers, saunas, or strenuous exercise for 24 hours.
  • Refrain from using makeup for the first 24 hours to allow your skin to breathe.
  • Follow your practitioner’s recommendations for any follow-up treatments.
