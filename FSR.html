This treatment Rejuvenates your skin from the outside improving skin texture increasing luminosity and reducing the appearance of lines and wrinkles. Perfect for crepey and sun-damaged skin on the face, neck, hands and décolleté.

How does it work?

EndyMed FSR is a great option for skin tightening, Radiofrequency is delivered to the skin in two different ways simultaneously, through micro-resurfacing and dermal heating. Micro-resurfacing is used to treat issues on the superficial skin layers of the epidermis, smoothing and brightening the complexion, whilst process will continue until the treatment reaches completion. EndyMed FSR is a safe, clinically proven treatment that can be used alone or in conjunction with other procedures to deliver a natural boost to the skin and rejuvenate from the deepest

All skin will be treated with a gentle Topical Anaesthetic Cream prior to the procedure to minimize any discomfort. Your practitioner will mark out a pattern on the skin to act as a guide for the EndyMed technology to aid the practitioner in precision. The treatment works by producing heat deep within the skin. This increases collagen formation while simultaneously treating the upper layers of the skin with pixelated heat spots which produces tissue regeneration.

BEFORE CARE

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for at least 2 weeks before treatment.
  • Refrain from using retinol, glycolic acid, or exfoliating products for 3-5 days prior.
  • Do not undergo hair removal treatments (waxing, threading, etc.) for at least 1 week prior.
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications (e.g., aspirin) for 1 week unless otherwise directed by a doctor.
  • Stay well-hydrated in the days leading up to your appointment.
  • Arrive with clean, makeup-free skin on the day of the treatment.
  • Discuss any allergies, medical conditions, or skin concerns with your provider in advance.
  • Notify your provider of any active infections, open wounds, or skin conditions in the treatment area.

AFTER CARE

  • Avoid washing your face or the treated area for at least 4 hours post-treatment.
  • Use a gentle, non-soap cleanser; avoid rubbing or scrubbing the treated skin.
  • Apply soothing, hydrating moisturizers as recommended by your provider.
  • Do not pick, peel, or scratch at any scabs or dry skin that may form.
  • Avoid direct sun exposure, and apply broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) when outdoors.
  • Refrain from using hot water, steam rooms, saunas, or engaging in intense physical activities for at least 1 week.
  • Do not use exfoliating products, retinol, or any harsh treatments until the skin has fully healed.
  • Limit the application of makeup for the first 24-48 hours post-treatment to allow the skin to breathe.
  • Maintain hydration to promote skin recovery and healing.
  • Contact your provider if you experience signs of irritation, swelling, or any other unexpected discomfort.
