Endymed:

ENDYMED TIGHTEN is suitable for all skin types and has an excellent safety profile. A pain-free treatment with no downtime that tightens and lifts lax skin on the cheek, jowl and neck area as well as around the eyes. Tailored treatment target specific areas of concern for the most effective results.

ENDYMED 3 DEEP RF TIGHTEN treatment use an advanced form of radiofrequency energy, 3DEEP Skin Science, to heat deep within the skin where collagen is formed.

How does it work?

After each treatment as your collagen contracts you will have an immediate lift and a healthy glow, the perfect pick-me up before a big event! Optimum results will be seen 3 months after your treatment course once the new collagen has been produced.

Heat causes an instant lift as your existing collagen contracts, but it also triggers a heat response in your skin that stimulates fibroblasts to produce new collagen, which is what gives you the natural, long-lasting results in skin tightening and lifting. The heat response also triggers other rejuvenating processes in your skin, improving its overall underlying structure and hydration.

What does the OXYGENEO facial offer?

Each treatment will last between 20 and 40 minutes depending on the area that you’re having treated, and it’s recommended that you follow a course of six treatments overall. You can then return for ‘top-up’ treatments to maintain and enhance the results that you’ve already seen

BEFORE CARE

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for at least 2 weeks.
  • Refrain from using retinol, glycolic acid, or exfoliating products for 3-5 days.
  • Do not undergo hair removal treatments for at least 1 week prior.
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications for 1 week.
  • Stay hydrated leading up to your appointment.
  • Arrive with clean, makeup-free skin.
  • Discuss any allergies or skin conditions with your provider.
  • Notify your provider about any active infections or open wounds.

AFTER CARE

  • Avoid washing your face for at least 4 hours post-treatment.
  • Use a gentle, non-soap cleanser; do not rub the treated area.
  • Apply a soothing, hydrating moisturizer as recommended.
  • Do not pick or peel at the skin.
  • Avoid sun exposure; use broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher).
  • Avoid hot water, steam rooms, saunas, or intense workouts for at least 1 week.
  • Do not use exfoliating products or retinol until fully healed.
  • Limit makeup application for 24-48 hours.
  • Stay hydrated to support skin recovery.
  • Contact your provider if you experience any signs of irritation or discomfort.
