PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) Therapy

Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) Therapy, otherwise known as PRP, is an all-natural means of rejuvenating the skin from wrinkles, textural imbalances, and more. PRP is created within a small sample of your own blood.

How does PRP work?

This advanced treatment uses the client's blood plasma, rich in growth factors, to rejuvenate the skin, promote healing, and enhance collagen production. PRP therapy is known for its effectiveness in improving skin texture, reducing fine lines and wrinkles, and promoting hair growth.

A small amount of blood is drawn from the individual into a sterile tube in exactly the same manner as a standard blood sample. The tube containing the blood is placed into a centrifuge and spun to separate the plasma and platelets from the other blood components. After a few minutes, the plasma and concentrated platelets are removed from the sample tube and re-introduced to the individual in the desired areas.


What parts of the body can be treated?

Areas commonly treated include:

  • Eyes
  • Face
  • Neck
  • Hands and arms
  • Scars
  • Stretch marks

Because the platelets are produced from the individual’s own, blood there is no risk of rejection or disease transmission. Research and clinical data show PRP is very safe with minimal risk of adverse reactions or complications. Improvement of skin texture and tone are noticeable within 3 weeks. Full collagen regeneration takes up to 3 months.

BEFORE CARE

  • Avoid taking blood thinners (such as aspirin, ibuprofen, or Vitamin E) for at least one week prior to treatment.
  • Do not consume alcohol 24 hours before the procedure.
  • Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water the day before your treatment.
  • Avoid any skincare treatments, including facials and chemical peels, for one week prior.
  • Consult your doctor if you are taking any medications or have medical conditions that may affect the procedure.

AFTER CARE

  • Avoid strenuous exercise for at least 24-48 hours after the treatment.
  • Refrain from applying makeup for at least 24 hours post-treatment.
  • Keep the treated area clean and moisturized; avoid touching or picking at the area.
  • Avoid direct sun exposure for at least a week after the procedure.
  • Stay hydrated and follow any additional recommendations from your practitioner.
  • Expect mild swelling or bruising in the treated area, which should subside within a few days.
