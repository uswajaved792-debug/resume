LIMELIGHT:

The Xeo LimeLight IPL photo facial by Cutera at Dermique Laser Clinic is a non-invasive skin rejuvenation facial used to reverse the signs of aging, sun damage and vascular concerns.

Cutera’s LimeLight IPL therapy delivers a non-invasive light treatment that can be customized for different skin tones and aging conditions, resulting in an exceptional skin revitalization treatment with minimal discomfort. In addition to treating sun spots, benign pigmented lesions, and vascular concerns. LimeLight also balances color and improves the complexion

How does imelight work?

LimeLight targets skin imperfections and skin damage associated with the natural aging process, environmental factors and photodamage to diminish the years of skin damage.

The XEO LimeLight IPL photo facial procedure carry out the following tasks:

  • Brown spots associated with sun damage
  • Benign brown pigmented lesion or age spots
  • Facial redness or rosacea, vascular lesions
  • Facial veins
  • Telangiectasia
  • Poikiloderma and mild to moderate inflammatory acne

BEFORE CARE

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for at least 4 weeks before treatment.
  • Stop using retinol, glycolic acid, or exfoliating products 1 week prior.
  • Do not undergo hair removal treatments (waxing, threading) on the treatment area for 1 week before.
  • Discontinue blood-thinning medications like aspirin or ibuprofen 1 week before (if approved by your doctor).
  • Ensure the treatment area is clean, free of makeup, lotions, or creams.
  • Inform your provider of any medical conditions, allergies, or skin sensitivities.

AFTER CARE

  • Avoid washing the treated area for at least 4 hours post-treatment.
  • Use a gentle, non-soap cleanser and avoid rubbing or scrubbing the skin.
  • Apply soothing, hydrating moisturizers as recommended by your provider.
  • Avoid direct sun exposure and use sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) for at least 4 weeks.
  • Refrain from using hot water, steam rooms, or saunas for at least 1 week.
  • Avoid using exfoliating products, retinol, or harsh treatments until fully healed.
  • Limit makeup application in the treated area for 24-48 hours.
  • Contact your provider if you experience unusual swelling, redness, or irritation.
