OXYGENEO GLOW & GO FACIAL

You will be treated with our Unique SKINOLOGIX LDN Diamond Facial for anti-aging and/or skin brightening treatment provides a unique approach to facial treatments.

Oxygeneo's face and neck skin oxygenation treatment is the only one to oxygenate the skin from within and simultaneously resurfaces the skin allowing for optimal absorption of revitalizing nutrients

How does it work?

This is an extremely gentle process that can be used even on the most sensitive of areas, such as around the eyes, without causing severe irritation. The special Gel used in this treatment contains Glycolic, Lactic & Mandelic active fruit acids that will be applied on the skin and the treatment will be performed using an applicator with a special capsule made of very mild form of Soda by Carbonate reacted on it. During the treatment the reaction between the capsule and the special gel will cause the infusion of the Active Ingredients. Essential nutrients are infused into the skin to promote healthy regeneration. Packed with useful active ingredients, each formulation can be customised to your unique skin type for the most targeted, beneficial treatments. After the first applicator has been used, another applicator, a high-tech disc cartridge is used. This applicator produces bubbles of carbon dioxide to create an environment that boosts oxygen levels, as well as stimulating blood flow and encouraging healthy production of newer skin cells. This not only improves overall skin health, but increases volume and hydration for a flawless complexion. The treatment is comfortable, painless, non invasive and does not require down time.

BEFORE CARE

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for at least 2 weeks before your treatment.
  • Refrain from using retinol, glycolic acid, or any exfoliating products for 3-5 days prior.
  • Do not use any hair removal treatments like waxing or laser for 1 week before the facial.
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications, such as aspirin or ibuprofen, for 1 week prior.
  • Stay hydrated and drink plenty of water in the days leading up to your appointment.
  • Arrive with clean, makeup-free skin.
  • Discuss any allergies or skin conditions with your provider prior to treatment.
  • If you have any active skin infections, notify your provider beforehand.

AFTER CARE

  • Avoid washing your face for the first 12-24 hours after the treatment.
  • Use a gentle, non-soap cleanser and avoid rubbing the skin.
  • Apply a hydrating moisturizer recommended by your provider to soothe the skin.
  • Avoid picking or peeling the skin; allow it to heal naturally.
  • Refrain from sun exposure and use broad-spectrum sunscreen daily (SPF 30 or higher).
  • Avoid hot water, steam rooms, saunas, or intense workouts for 1 week.
  • Do not use exfoliating products, retinol, or acid-based products until fully healed.
  • Limit makeup application for 24-48 hours after the treatment.
  • Stay hydrated and drink plenty of water to aid recovery.
